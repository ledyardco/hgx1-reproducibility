# HG-X1 Pilot Report - 2026-10-02

Status: **pilot only**. This is not the official HG-X1 measurement, not peer review, and not empirical support for the public research claim.

## Purpose

Validate the HG-X1 run path on a small sample before freezing a full official experiment.

## Pilot design

- Corpus slice: first 10 BullshitBench v2 questions by source order.
- Repeats: k = 1.
- Arm A: raw `gpt-oss-120b` through Cerebras, direct-answer instruction, temperature 0.
- Arm C: `genxis/gavel-answer` through the Gavel API, temperature 0.
- Scoring: deterministic pilot proxy only: explicit abstain/refusal or direct premise challenge keyword.
- Not used: canonical BullshitBench judge panel, controls, k=3 repeats, official binding gates.

## What this pilot can and cannot say

Can say: the pilot harness executed both arms on 10 questions, recorded transcripts, produced a deterministic proxy table, and found no transport errors.

Cannot say: HG-X1 is proven, the public claim is empirically supported, Arm C is generally superior, or the result is reproducible as an official benchmark. The official run still requires a frozen protocol, subject-model pin, controls, k=3 repeats, canonical judging or predeclared scorer, and binding receipts.
