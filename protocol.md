# HG-X1 Protocol Draft

## Primary claim
Verification plus abstention reduces unsupported assertions versus fluent baseline answers on the same question set.

## Arms
- Arm A: Baseline direct answer.
- Arm B: Retrieval with citations, without claim-level enforcement.
- Arm C: Verification plus abstention: unsupported claims are removed or abstained.

## Metrics
- Unsupported assertion rate.
- Verified-claim coverage.
- Abstention rate.
- Correct answers withheld.
- Verifier failure rate.
- Cost and latency.

## Publication rule
Results are not public until scripts, prompts, model pins, transcript outputs, failure cases, and receipt-backed result tables are published together.
